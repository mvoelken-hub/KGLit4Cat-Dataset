# Past, Present and Future of Heterogeneous Catalysis

**Authors:** Ioana Fechete, Ye Wang, Jacques C. Védrine
**Year:** 2012
**Venue:** Catalysis Today 189: 2-27
**File:** `docs/literature/1-s2.0-S0920586112002751-main.pdf`

## Abstract
Review of key catalytic discoveries and industrial processes over 300 years. Covers commodities, fine chemicals, petrochemicals, petroleum transformation, emission control. Discusses catalyst characterization, high selectivity, and future challenges (environment, energy, pollution, biomass, water splitting).

## Key Findings
- 90% of chemical processes involve catalysts in at least one step
- >60% of 63 major products and 90% of 34 process innovations (1930-1980) involved catalysis
- Historical driving forces: wars, embargos → now market-driven, environmental
- Future challenges: environmental issues, energy supply, pollution control, H₂ sources, CO₂ storage/upgrading, biomass, water splitting
