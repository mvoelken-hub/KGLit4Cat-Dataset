# From Text to Insight: LLMs for Chemical Data Extraction

**Authors:** Mara Schilling-Wilhelmi, Martín Ríos-García, Sherjeel Shabih, et al.
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** Chem. Soc. Rev. 54: 1125–1150
**File:** `docs/literature/d4cs00913d.pdf`

## Abstract
Tutorial review providing comprehensive overview of LLM-based structured data extraction in chemistry. Covers end-to-end workflows, advanced techniques (multimodal approaches, agentic systems), quality assurance (constrained decoding, domain-specific validation), and future frontiers.

## Key Findings
- End-to-end workflow: data collection → structured output
- Advanced techniques: multimodal approaches, agentic systems
- Quality assurance: constrained decoding, domain-specific validation
- Synergy between LLMs and chemical expertise
- Future: cross-document analysis, diverse modalities, emerging challenges
- Serves as foundational resource for LLM-based chemical data extraction
