# Ontologies4Cat

**Authors:** Alexander S. Behr, Hendrik Borgelt, Norbert Kockmann (TU Dortmund)
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** Journal of Cheminformatics 16:16
**File:** `docs/literature/s13321-024-00807-2.pdf`

## Abstract
Systematic collection of ontology metadata with focus on catalysis research data value chain. Classifies ontologies by subdomains of catalysis research, enabling efficient comparison. Presents workflow and codebase for metadata representation on GitHub, plus automatic mapping of ontology classes.

## Key Findings
- Ontology landscape for catalysis: systematic collection, classification, and comparison
- Classification by subdomains of catalysis research data value chain
- Automatic mapping of ontology classes for relatedness analysis
- Reusable methodology — adaptable to other domains
- Quality metrics for ontology reusability

## Relevance
Comprehensive ontology survey for catalysis. Relevant to [[catalysis-ontologies]], [[ontology-resources]], and [[nfdi4cat]]. https://github.com/nfdi4cat/Ontology-Overview-of-NFDI4Cat
