# Knowledge Graphs in Heterogeneous Catalysis — Review

**Authors:** Raúl Díaz, Hongliang Xin (Virginia Tech)
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** Chinese Journal of Chemical Engineering 84: 179-189
**File:** `docs/literature/1-s2.0-S1004954125002393-main.pdf`

## Abstract
Reviews recent developments in catalysis KGs, techniques for graph construction, and how AI (particularly LLMs) enhances graph generation and query. Systematic analysis covering ontology-guided text mining, graph population, and maintenance. Identifies key trends and challenges.

## Key Findings
- KGs provide semantic framework supporting FAIR data integration in catalysis
- Ontology-based approaches enable automated extraction of domain knowledge
- LLM-driven RAG supports natural-language queries over KGs
- Scalable architectures: from thousands to >1M triples
- Applications: catalyst recommendation systems, reaction mechanism discovery
- Challenges: data heterogeneity, ontology alignment, long-term curation
- Community standards for ontology development and maintenance needed
