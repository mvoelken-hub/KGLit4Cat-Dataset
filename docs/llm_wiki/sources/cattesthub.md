# CatTestHub

**Authors:** Atharva S. Burte, Advaith Nair, Lars C. Grabow, Paul J. Dauenhauer, Susannah L. Scott, Omar A. Abdelrahman
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** J. Catal.
**File:** `docs/literature/1-s2.0-S0021951724006158-am.pdf`

## Abstract
Open-access database dedicated to benchmarking experimental heterogeneous catalysis data. Combines systematically reported catalytic activity data for selected probe chemistries with material characterization and reactor configuration. Currently spans 250+ experimental data points, 24 solid catalysts, 3 distinct catalytic chemistries. Balances FAIR data design principles with fundamental information needs.

## Key Findings
- Quantitative comparison of catalytic materials hindered by variability in reaction conditions, reporting procedures
- 250+ unique experimental data points, 24 solid catalysts, 3 probe chemistries
- Open-access platform for community-wide benchmarking
- Roadmap for expansion through community contributions
