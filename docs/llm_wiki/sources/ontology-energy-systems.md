# Ontology-Driven Dataspaces for Energy Systems

**Authors:** Kai Heussen, Jawad Kazmi, et al. (DTU / AIT / ZHAW / UCD)
**Year:** 2026
**Venue:** arXiv (eess.SY)
**File:** `docs/literature/towards-reproducible-test-annotation-for-cyber-physical-energy-systems-using-ontology-driven-dataspaces-2604.19686v1.pdf`

## Abstract
Ontology-driven approach for reproducibility of cyber-physical energy systems testing. Machine-actionable descriptions via structured ontologies. Proposes open three-viewpoint ontology framework. Uses CIM, SAREF, SEAS, Brick, QUDT ontologies.

Adjacent domain ontology application. Relevant to [[ontology-development]] and [[fair-data-for-catalysis]] (cross-domain parallels).
