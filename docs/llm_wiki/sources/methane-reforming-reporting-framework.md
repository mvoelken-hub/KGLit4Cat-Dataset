**Authors:** Ismail Salim, Abdallah S. Berrouk (Khalifa University)
**Year:** 2026
**Venue:** npj Computational Materials
**File:** `docs/literature/s41524-025-01953-3_reference.pdf`

## Abstract
Framework and review for standardized scientific data reporting in catalytic methane reforming. Proposes structured reporting standards to make catalysis data more machine-readable and reusable.
