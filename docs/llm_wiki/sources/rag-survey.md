# RAG Survey

**Authors:** Yunfan Gao, Yun Xiong, Xinyu Gao, et al.
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** arXiv survey
**File:** `docs/literature/2312.10997v5.pdf`

## Abstract
Surveys retrieval-augmented generation as a way to address hallucination, outdated knowledge, and opaque reasoning in LLMs. Organizes RAG into naive, advanced, and modular paradigms and reviews retrieval, generation, augmentation, evaluation, and open challenges.

## Key Findings
- RAG joins LLM parametric knowledge with external, updateable knowledge bases.
- The field has moved from simple retrieve-then-generate pipelines toward modular RAG systems.
- Retrieval, generation, and augmentation each introduce design and evaluation choices.
- Evaluation must cover factuality, relevance, faithfulness, and task-specific utility.
