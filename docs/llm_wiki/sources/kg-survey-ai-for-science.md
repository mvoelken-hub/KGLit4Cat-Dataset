# Knowledge Graphs in AI for Science — Survey

**Authors:** Keyan Ding, Zhihui Zhu, Yuqi Tang, et al. (Zhejiang University, Shanghai AI Lab)
**Year:** 2026
**Venue:** National Science Review 13: nwag140
**File:** `docs/literature/nwag140.pdf`

## Abstract
Comprehensive survey of scientific knowledge graphs (SciKGs) covering construction methodologies and applications across biology, chemistry, and materials science. Examines how SciKGs support drug development, omics analysis, reaction prediction, and materials design. Highlights synergistic integration of SciKGs and LLMs.

## Key Findings
- SciKGs as foundational knowledge infrastructure for AI for science
- LLMs as dynamic semantic engines working with SciKGs
- Applications: drug development, omics, reaction prediction, materials design
- Vision: self-updating graphs co-evolving with LLMs, embodied within AI scientists
- Key challenges: auditable, interoperable, self-evolving SciKGs
