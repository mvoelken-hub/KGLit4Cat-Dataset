# Epicure: Food Ingredient Embeddings

**Authors:** Jakub Radzikowski, Josef Chen (KAIKAKU.AI)
**Year:** 2026
**Venue:** arXiv (cs.AI)
**File:** `docs/literature/2605.22391v1.pdf`

## Abstract
Family of skip-gram ingredient embeddings from multilingual recipe corpus. Aggregates 4.14M recipes from 11 sources spanning 7 languages. Three sibling models (Cooc, Chem, Core) differ only in random-walk schema, exposing chemistry-vs-recipe-context as controllable design axis.

## Relevance
Peripheral — food/chemistry embeddings. May be relevant to [[chemical-llms]] for embedding methodology, but domain is food science, not catalysis.
