# Marconato PhD Thesis: Reasoning Shortcuts

**Author:** Emanuele Marconato
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** PhD Thesis, National Ph.D. in AI, Università di Pisa / Università di Trento (XXXVII cycle)
**File:** `docs/literature/phd_thesis_marconato_final_2.pdf`

## Abstract
Analyzes how to learn concepts from data such that they possess correct semantics. Two major contributions: (1) explaining and addressing pitfalls in learning concepts for reasoning tasks — Reasoning Shortcuts where models leverage poor-quality concepts for correct predictions; (2) formal framework to test concept quality, and models that boost concept quality via advanced representation learning.

## Key Findings
- Reasoning Shortcuts: models achieve correct predictions using poor-quality concepts
- Formal framework for testing quality of learned concepts
- BEARS: makes neuro-symbolic models aware of their reasoning shortcuts
- CLE4EVR benchmark for systematically evaluating reasoning shortcuts
- Analysis of identifiable linear properties of next-token predictors in language modeling

## Relevance
Theoretical foundations for concept learning and reasoning shortcuts. Relevant to [[chemical-llms]] (concept quality in domain-specific models) and [[automated-scientific-discovery]] (trustworthy AI reasoning).
