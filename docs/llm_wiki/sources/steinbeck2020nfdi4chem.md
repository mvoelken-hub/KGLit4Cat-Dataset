# NFDI4Chem Grant Proposal

**Authors:** Christoph Steinbeck, Oliver Koepler, Felix Bach, et al.
**Year:** 2020
**Venue:** Res. Ideas Outcomes 6: e55852
**File:** `docs/literature/RIO_article_55852.pdf`

## Abstract
Grant proposal for NFDI4Chem — the chemistry consortium within Germany's NFDI. Vision: digitalisation of all key steps in chemical research. Six key objectives: federated repositories, international standards, Smart Lab environments, community engagement, cross-consortia synergies, and legal frameworks for FAIR data.

## Key Findings
- Six key objectives for NFDI4Chem
- Key Objective 1: Federated repositories for storing, disclosing, searching, re-using data
- Key Objective 2: International minimum information (MI) standards and open data standards
- Key Objective 3: Smart Laboratory Environments (ELN, digital tools)
- Key Objective 4: Community engagement and FAIR data management adoption
- Key Objective 5: Synergies with other consortia (e.g., NFDI4Cat)
- Key Objective 6: Legal framework for FAIR and open RDM
