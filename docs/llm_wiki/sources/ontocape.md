# OntoCAPE

**Authors:** Wolfgang Marquardt, Jan Morbach, Andreas Wiesner, Aidong Yang
**Year:** 2010
**Venue:** RWTH Aachen / Book
**File:** `docs/literature/marquardt2010.pdf`

## Abstract
Re-usable ontology for chemical process engineering. Provides formal representation of chemical processes, materials, equipment, and methods for use in computer-aided process engineering.

## Relevance
Domain ontology for chemical engineering. Relevant to [[ontology-development]] and [[catalysis-ontologies]]. Predecessor influence on catalysis ontology efforts.
