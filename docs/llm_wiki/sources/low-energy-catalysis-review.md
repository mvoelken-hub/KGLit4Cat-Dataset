# Low-Energy Catalytic Processes Review

**Authors:** Ahmed I. Osman, Ali Ayati, Pavel Krivoshapkin, et al.
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** Coordination Chemistry Reviews 514: 215900
**File:** `docs/literature/1-s2.0-S0010854524002467-main.pdf`

## Abstract
Review of low-energy catalytic processes for sustainable chemical production. Covers enzyme mimicking, biodiesel production, CO₂ capture, organic synthesis. Emphasizes single-atom catalysis (SAC) and diatomic catalysts (DACs) for low-energy catalysis. Coordination chemistry principles in catalyst design.

## Key Findings
- Low-energy catalysis: milder conditions, enhanced selectivity, catalyst recyclability
- SACs and DACs: exceptional performance at minimal activation energies
- Coordination chemistry: tailoring ligand properties (steric/electronic effects) for catalyst design
- Electronic structure modulation via ligand design influences reactivity, selectivity, stability
- Applications: enzyme mimicking, biodiesel, CO₂ capture, water treatment, organic synthesis
