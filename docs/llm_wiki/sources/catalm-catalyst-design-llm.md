# CataLM: Empowering Catalyst Design Through Large Language Models

**Authors:** Ludi Wang, Xueqing Chen, Yi Du, Yuanchun Zhou, Yang Gao, Wenjuan Cui
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** arXiv (cs.LG)
**File:** `docs/literature/catalm-empowering-catalyst-design-through-large-language-models-2405.17440v1.pdf`

## Abstract
Introduces CataLM, a large language model fine-tuned for the domain of electrocatalytic materials. Demonstrates potential for facilitating human-AI collaboration in catalyst knowledge exploration and design. Claims to be the first LLM dedicated to the catalyst domain.

## Key Findings
- Fine-tuning open-source LLMs on catalysis literature yields domain-specific capabilities
- CataLM supports catalyst knowledge exploration and design through conversational interaction
- Addresses the gap that general LLMs lack reliable catalyst knowledge (structural features, performance analyses absent from common pre-training corpora)

## Methodology
- Fine-tuning approach (not RAG) on domain-specific catalysis literature
- Focused on electrocatalytic materials domain

## Relevance
One of the first domain-specialized LLMs for catalysis. Relevant to [[chemical-llms]] and [[llm-guided-catalyst-discovery]].
