# NFDI4Cat White Paper: Ontology-Based Data Management

**Authors:** NFDI4Cat Consortium (workshop November 2023)
**Year:** 2024
**Venue:** White Paper
**File:** `docs/literature/NFDI4Cat_Whitepaper-2024-04-26.pdf`

## Abstract
Collective insights on harnessing ontologies and tools for catalysis research data. Covers literature search, experimental/simulation data entry, analysis, storage, and research planning. Demonstrates knowledge graphs for structured FAIR data handling.

## Key Findings
- Ontologies + domain-specific vocabulary + standard metadata → knowledge graphs
- Applications: literature search, experimental data entry, simulation data, analysis, storage, research planning
- Tools mentioned: Lara Suite, ELN FURTHRmind, Digital Labs, NOMAD, ADACTA, Cross Domain Meta Ontology
- Catering to different skill levels (researchers, advanced users, experts)

## Relevance
Practical guide for ontology-based RDM in catalysis. Relevant to [[nfdi4cat]], [[catalysis-data-infrastructure]], and [[catalysis-ontologies]].
