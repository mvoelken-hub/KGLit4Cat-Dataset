# Modeling Time-On-Stream Catalyst Reactivity with AI

**Authors:** Jonathan M. Mauß, Klara S. Kley, et al. (Fritz Haber Institute)
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** ACS Catalysis 15: 12652-12665
**File:** `docs/literature/modeling-time-on-stream-catalyst-reactivity-in-the-selective-hydrogenation-of-concentrated-acetylene-streams-under.pdf`

## Abstract
AI approach for modeling time-on-stream (tOS) dependent reactivity of Pd-based bimetallic catalysts. Uses subgroup discovery and SISSO symbolic regression. Applied to selective hydrogenation of concentrated acetylene streams under industrial conditions. AI models enable design of bimetallic/trimetallic catalysts, experimentally validated.

## Key Findings
- Subgroup discovery identifies descriptions of materials/conditions resulting in noticeable acetylene conversion
- SISSO symbolic regression models time-dependent selectivity at high conversion
- AI identifies key physicochemical descriptors correlated with reactivity
- Critical interplay between material structure and chemical potential of reaction mixture
- Experimental validation of AI-designed catalysts
