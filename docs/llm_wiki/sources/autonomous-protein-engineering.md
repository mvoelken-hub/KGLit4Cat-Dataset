# Autonomous Protein Engineering

**Authors:** Konstantin FG Weigmann, Uwe T Bornscheuer, Mark Doerr (University of Greifswald)
**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** Current Opinion in Biotechnology 97: 103395
**File:** `docs/literature/1-s2.0-S0958166925001399-main.pdf`

## Abstract
Review of autonomous protein engineering platforms. Covers Design-Build-Test-Learn (DBTL) cycle, AI in Design/Learn phases, automation, and closed-loop optimization. Envisions distributed self-driving laboratories with FAIR data principles and knowledge graphs.

## Key Findings
- Zero-shot protein language models predict beneficial mutations from sequence data
- Supervised models integrate assay results to refine variant designs
- Closed-loop optimization: library design → automated expression → assays → ML decision-making
- Engineering cycles reduced from months to days
- Orchestration frameworks with FAIR data + KGs for distributed self-driving labs
- Open-source and modular alternatives emerging
