# A Survey of Large Language Models

**Authors:** Wayne Xin Zhao, Kun Zhou, Junyi Li, et al.
**Year:** 2026
**Venue:** Frontiers of Computer Science
**File:** `docs/literature/s11704-026-60308-3.pdf`

## Abstract
Systematic survey of LLM development across pre-training, post-training, utilization, and evaluation. It reviews model architecture, data, alignment, prompting, agentic reasoning, benchmarks, safety, and open research challenges.

## Key Findings
- Organizes LLM progress into pre-training, post-training, utilization, and evaluation.
- Treats prompt engineering, in-context learning, and agents as deployment strategies.
- Highlights alignment, scaling efficiency, evaluation, and agentic capability as open issues.
- Useful as a broad map for interpreting specialized LLM literature.
